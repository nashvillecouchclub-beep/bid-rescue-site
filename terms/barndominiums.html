Client terms

Our terms, in plain words

This page is the whole deal between your company ("you") and Bid Rescue ("we").

1. What we do

You send us your unsold estimates. We write follow up emails in your style, you click one button to approve them, and they go out from your own business email to people who asked you for a price. We pass warm replies to you. We do not promise any number of signed jobs.

2. Our role: your outside vendor

  • We are an independent company, not your employee. We act as your vendor and agent, in your business name, only for the work in your plan.
  • You keep your own trade licenses and do all of your trade work. We never sell, price or negotiate a job, sign a contract, or take a deposit.
  • We do not give legal, insurance or hiring advice.

3. Our 60 day guarantee

On any plan with a monthly fee: if no job signs in your first 60 days, your next month's fee is free. It applies only when you gave us 25 or more valid estimates and approved your emails within 7 days of receiving them. Once per client. Pay per signed job plans have no monthly fee and are not part of it.

4. Your area is yours

On the exclusive plan, we work with only one company in your trade in your metro area while you are with us. Your area is set by where your business is based; jobs you take outside it still count. If you cancel, your area opens to the next company on our waitlist.

That means no competitor in your area can use us against you. You never fight another company in your trade over the same customers we bring back, and every warm reply is yours alone.

5. Your list is yours

You own your customer list. We use it only to deliver your plan. We never sell it, share it, or use it for another company. When you cancel, we stop emailing your list and delete it on request, except what we must keep for billing records.

6. Who we email, and how

  • Only people on your list who asked you for an estimate or a price. You confirm you have the right to contact them.
  • Every email has a true subject line, your business mailing address and a clear way to opt out, and we honor opt outs right away.
  • Email only. We do not call or text.
  • In some states we offer flat monthly plans only and the emails only invite the customer to reply or book with you.

7. Case studies and references

We share your results as a case study, or ask you to be a reference, only if you say yes in writing. You can change your mind any time.

8. When a job counts as signed

A job counts only when someone we emailed for you signs within 12 months of our first email to them. You choose who goes on your list; anyone you leave off, and anyone who signs before our first email reaches them, never counts. We send you an email when someone is ready; you tap signed to confirm. Nothing is charged for a job until you tap signed. Until you answer, we email you a quick one tap check in (Signed, Still working on it, or Lost) a few days after each warm lead and then every couple of weeks, so no job slips through. The Accept button on a customer page only tells you they are ready.

9. Cancel

  • Monthly plans have no contract and cancel by email.
  • Exclusive plans cancel any time with 30 days notice.
  • Fees are still owed on jobs from people we emailed for you that sign within the 12 month window, even after you cancel.

10. How you pay

  • You save a card on Square's secure page at sign up. We never see or store your card number.
  • Saving your card costs nothing today. Monthly plans: your first fee is charged 30 days after we start, once your first month of work is done, and then every 30 days. Per job fees are charged only after you tap signed, in three parts as your customer pays you.
  • We email you 3 to 4 days before each charge: nothing to pay, the date, and what it covers.
  • Every charge, of any size, goes through automatically. You do not need to approve it.
  • You get a detailed receipt after every charge.
  • If a charge fails, we tell you right away and try again.

11. Fees

Exclusive plan

$995 a month

Locked in for as long as you stay. Only our first 3 barndominium builders nationwide ever get this price. After that it is $2,950 a month.

Every estimate followed up, old and new, plus win back and your area lock.

Per job fees when a job signs:

  • $100k to $250k$1,950
  • Over $250k$3,500

If you cancel and come back, the regular price applies.

Pay per signed job

No monthly fee. Covers one batch of your old unsold estimates, up to 12 months old, followed up once. Does not include new estimates, win back or the area lock. Based on the signed contract price.

  • $100k to $250k$3,500
  • Over $250k$5,950

A job outside these sizes has its fee written in your order before we start. Not offered in states where our rules allow flat monthly plans only.

Ways to save (pick one, they do not stack)

  • Sign within 7 days of your free check: half off your first month or first fee
  • Prepay 3 months: 10% off
  • Prepay a year: 2 months free

12. How per job fees are charged

  • 30% when your customer pays their deposit
  • 40% at their middle payment
  • 30% at their final payment
  • No update from you? The second part is charged 45 days after signing and the last part 90 days after signing.
  • We never touch your customer's money. It goes straight to you.
