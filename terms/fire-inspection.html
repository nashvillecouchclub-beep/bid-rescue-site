Client terms

Our terms, in plain words

This page is the whole deal between your company ("you") and Bid Rescue ("we").

1. What we do

You send us your open deficiency quotes. We write follow up emails in your style, you click one button to approve them, and they go out from your own business email to building owners and managers who got a quote from you. We pass warm replies to you. We do not promise any number of approved repairs.

2. Our role: your outside vendor

  • We are an independent company, not your employee. We act as your vendor and agent, in your business name, only for the work in your plan.
  • You keep your own trade licenses and do all of your trade work. We never sell, price or negotiate a job, sign a contract, or take a deposit.
  • We do not give legal, insurance or hiring advice.

3. Our 60 day guarantee

On any plan with a monthly fee: if no repair is approved in your first 60 days, your next month's fee is free. It applies only when you gave us 25 or more valid deficiency quotes and approved your emails within 7 days of receiving them. Once per client. Pay per signed job plans have no monthly fee and are not part of it.

4. Your list is yours

You own your customer list. We use it only to deliver your plan. We never sell it, share it, or use it for another company. When you cancel, we stop emailing your list and delete it on request, except what we must keep for billing records.

5. Who we email, and how

  • Only building owners and managers on your list who got a deficiency quote from you. You confirm you have the right to contact them.
  • Every email has a true subject line, your business mailing address and a clear way to opt out, and we honor opt outs right away.
  • Email only. We do not call or text.
  • In some states we offer flat monthly plans only and the emails only invite the customer to reply or book with you.

6. Case studies and references

We share your results as a case study, or ask you to be a reference, only if you say yes in writing. You can change your mind any time.

7. Cancel

Your plan has no contract and cancels by email.

8. How you pay

You save a card on Square's secure page at sign up. We never see or store your card number. Nothing is charged until your first paid month or stage is delivered. Each charge then happens on its own under these terms. We email you 3 to 4 days before each charge (nothing to pay, the date, and what it covers) and send a detailed receipt after. Every charge, of any size, goes through automatically; you do not need to approve it. If a charge fails, we tell you right away and try again.

9. Fees

Your fee: Deficiency Rescue, $299 per inspector a month.

Sign up offers (pick one, they do not stack): half off your first month or first fee if you sign within 7 days of your free check; 10% off if you prepay 3 months; 2 months free if you prepay a year.

10. Questions
