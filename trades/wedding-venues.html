Questions venues ask

Do I need new software?

No software to buy, nothing to learn. You forward inquiries to the address we give you and share your open dates by a view only calendar link or a simple list.

Do you text couples or log in to my accounts?

No. Email and web form only. For Instagram we write the reply and you paste it.

When is a wedding counted as booked?

When you tap booked in the email we send you. The Accept button on a couple's page only tells you they are ready. We never touch a couple's deposit; it goes straight to you.

How is the per wedding fee charged?

Half when the couple pays their deposit to you, half when their final payment clears.

Which couples do you contact?

Inquiries from the last 6 to 12 months whose date is at least 4 months away. We skip passed dates and couples who booked elsewhere.
