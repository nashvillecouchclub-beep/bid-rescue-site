Why do you need us?

You are the owner, the estimator and the crew. Following up on every estimate is one more job, and it is the one that gets skipped.

  • You already paid for every estimate you sent. Leads, the visit and your evening writing it up all cost money. Too many of those estimates never get a second email. We write one for every new estimate.
  • You are working, not writing emails. When you are on a job all day, yesterday's estimates slip. We follow up on each one while you work.
