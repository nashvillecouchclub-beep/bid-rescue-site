Straight answers

Questions deck builders ask

Who sends the emails?

You do, in your name. We write each email in your style, you approve them all with one click, and it goes out from your own business email. Replies come straight to you, and we flag the warm ones the same day.

What do you need from me?

Your unsold estimates. Forward them to the address we give you, or upload a spreadsheet or CSV. A photo of the binder works too. No software to buy, nothing to learn.

What counts as a signed job?

A homeowner we emailed for you who signs within 12 months of our first email to them. You tap signed in the email we send you. We never charge for a job until you tap signed.

How does the per job fee get charged?

In three parts that follow your homeowner's payments to you: 30% when they pay their deposit, 40% at the middle payment, 30% at the final. If we hear nothing, the second part is charged 45 days after signing and the last part at 90 days. We never touch your homeowner's deposit; it goes straight to you.

What does exclusive mean?

One company per trade in each metro. While you are with us, we will not work for your competitors in your area. Cancel any time with 30 days notice. If your metro is taken, we put you on the waitlist.

What if someone was already about to sign?

Tell us before we start. Anyone you are already working with comes off the list, and we will not email them.

Is my customer list safe?

Your list stays yours. We use it only to follow up for you. We never sell it, share it, or use it for another company.

Do you call or text my customers?

No. Email only. Every email has a clear way to opt out and shows your business address.

When do I pay?

You save a card on Square's secure page when you sign up. Saving your card costs nothing today. Monthly plans: your first fee is charged 30 days after we start, once your first month of work is done, and then every 30 days. Per job fees are charged only after you tap signed, in three parts as your customer pays you. We email you 3 to 4 days before each charge, and a receipt after.

How do I cancel?

Send us an email. Monthly plans have no contract; exclusive plans need 30 days notice. Fees are still owed on jobs from people we emailed for you that sign within the 12 month window.
