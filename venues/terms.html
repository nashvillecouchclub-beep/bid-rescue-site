Client terms

Our terms, in plain words

This page is the whole deal between your venue ("you") and Bid Rescue ("we").

1. What we do

You send us your wedding inquiries that never booked. We write follow up emails in your style, you approve them with one click, and they go out from your own business email to couples who asked you about a date or a price. We pass warm replies to you. We do not promise any number of booked weddings.

2. Our role: your outside vendor

  • We are an independent company, not your employee. We act as your vendor and agent, in your venue's name, only for the work in your plan.
  • You keep your own licenses and run every event yourself. We never sell, price or negotiate a wedding, sign a contract, or take a deposit.
  • We do not give legal, insurance or hiring advice.

3. Our 60 day guarantee

On any plan with a monthly fee: if no wedding books in your first 60 days, your next month's fee is free. It applies only when you gave us 25 or more valid inquiries and approved your emails within 7 days of receiving them. Once per venue. The pay per booked wedding plan has no monthly fee and is not part of it.

4. Your area is yours

Only the exclusive plan is exclusive. Pay per booked wedding is not. If another venue in your area takes the exclusive plan, pay per booking is no longer open to you: we stop sending new emails for you within 7 days, and fees still apply on weddings that book from emails we already sent. On the exclusive plan, we work with only one wedding venue in your metro area while you are with us. Your area is set by where your venue is based; weddings from couples outside it still count. If you cancel, your area opens to the next venue on our waitlist.

That means no nearby venue can use us against you. You never compete with another venue for the same couples we bring back, and every couple is yours alone.

5. Your list is yours

You own your inquiry list. We use it only to deliver your plan. We never sell it, share it, or use it for another company. When you cancel, we stop emailing your list and delete it on request, except what we must keep for billing records.

6. Who we email, and how

  • Only couples on your list who asked you about a date or a price. You confirm you have the right to contact them.
  • Every email has a true subject line, your business mailing address and a clear way to opt out, and we honor opt outs right away.
  • Email only. We do not call or text.
  • In some states we offer flat monthly plans only and the emails only invite the couple to reply or book a tour with you.

7. Case studies and references

We share your results as a case study, or ask you to be a reference, only if you say yes in writing. You can change your mind any time.

8. When a wedding counts as booked

A wedding counts only when a couple we emailed for you books within 12 months of our first email to them. You choose who goes on your list; anyone you leave off, and any couple who books before our first email reaches them, never counts. We send you an email when a couple is ready; you tap Booked to confirm. Nothing is charged for a wedding until you tap Booked. Until you answer, we email you a quick one tap check in (Booked, Still deciding, or Lost) a few days after each warm lead and then every couple of weeks, so no wedding slips through. The Accept button on a couple's page only tells you they are ready.

9. Cancel

Monthly plans have no contract and cancel by email. Exclusive plans cancel any time with 30 days notice. Fees are still owed on weddings from couples we emailed for you that book within the 12 month window, even after you cancel.

10. How you pay

You save a card on Square's secure page at sign up. We never see or store your card number. Saving your card costs nothing today. On the exclusive plan, your first monthly fee is charged 30 days after we start, once your first month of work is done, and then every 30 days. Wedding fees are charged only after a couple books: half when they pay their deposit, half when their final payment clears. Each charge then happens on its own under these terms. We email you 3 to 4 days before each charge (nothing to pay, the date, and what it covers) and send a detailed receipt after. Every charge, of any size, goes through automatically; you do not need to approve it. If a charge fails, we tell you right away and try again.

11. Fees

Your fees: the exclusive plan: our first 3 wedding venues nationwide pay $395 a month, locked in for as long as they stay; after that it is $1,250 a month, plus $375 per booked wedding. Or no monthly fee and $995 per booked wedding, which covers Couple Comeback only (bringing back couples who inquired or toured and went quiet), and has no exclusivity: we can also work with other venues in your area. Only the exclusive plan, the full package, makes you the only venue we work with in your area. Wedding fees are charged half at the couple's deposit and half when their final payment clears.

First 3 price. Our first 3 wedding venues nationwide get the lower price shown on the venue price page. It is a monthly price, not a first month deal, and it never goes up while you stay. If you cancel and come back, you pay the regular price, if your area is still open.

Sign up offers (pick one, they do not stack): half off your first month or first fee if you sign within 7 days of your free inquiry check; 10% off if you prepay 3 months; 2 months free if you prepay a year.

12. How wedding fees are charged

Each booked wedding fee is charged in two parts: half when the couple pays their deposit, half when their final payment clears. We never touch the couple's money; it goes straight to you.
