Why do you need us?

You run beautiful events. Chasing every couple is work of its own, and it is the part that gets skipped.

  1. You already paid to get those inquiries, and most of them go quiet. Listings, ads, open houses and tours all cost money and time. We write to every couple who went quiet, so that money keeps working for you.
  2. You do not have time to chase every couple while you run events. Weekends go to weddings, weekdays go to tours and vendors. We keep the follow ups going every week, even on your busiest Saturday.
  3. A follow up that sounds pushy loses the couple. Writing a note that feels warm, not desperate, takes skill. We write each one around the date and the day they dreamed about.
  4. Couples book whoever answers first and keeps in touch. We answer new inquiries quickly with your open dates, and check back until the couple books or tells you no.
  5. Software still needs someone to run it. A CRM can send reminders, but someone still has to write them and keep up every week.
  6. We do all of it in your style, from your email, and you approve with one click. Nothing to learn. You read each set of emails, approve it with one click, and couples reply straight to you.
