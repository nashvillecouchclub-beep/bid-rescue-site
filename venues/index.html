Questions venues ask

Do I need new software?

No software to buy, nothing to learn. You forward inquiries to the address we give you and share your open dates by a view only calendar link or a simple list.

Who sends the emails?

You do, in your venue's name. We write each email in your style, you approve them with one click, and they go out from your own business email. Couples reply straight to you.

Do you text couples or sign in to my accounts?

No. Email and web form only. For Instagram we write the reply and you paste it.

When is a wedding counted as booked?

When you tap Booked in the email we send you. Until then we send a quick one tap check in (Booked, Still deciding, or Lost) a few days after each warm couple, then every couple of weeks. The Accept button on a couple's page only tells you they are ready. We never touch a couple's deposit; it goes straight to you.

How is the per wedding fee charged?

Half when the couple pays their deposit to you, half when their final payment clears.

Which couples do you write to?

Inquiries from the last 6 to 12 months whose date is at least 4 months away. We skip passed dates and couples who booked elsewhere.

What does one venue per area mean?

While you are on the exclusive plan, we will not work for another wedding venue in your area. Cancel any time with 30 days notice.
