Straight answers

Questions builders ask

Who sends the emails?

We do the work. We write the emails, send them, watch for replies and opens, and pass warm homeowners to you.

Whose name is on the emails?

Yours. Emails go out under your company name and the sending name you pick. Replies come to your own reply email. The homeowner hears from you, not from us.

What counts as a signed job?

A job counts when a homeowner from your list taps Accept on their proposal page, or when you tell us they signed. The fee is based on the signed contract price.

How long does a homeowner count?

Twelve months. If a homeowner from your list signs within 12 months of our first message to them, it counts as a signed job.

What does one builder per metro mean?

We work with only one pool builder in each metro. Once you are in, we will not work with your competitors in your area.

What if a homeowner was already about to sign?

Tell us before we start. Anyone you are already working with can come off the list, and we will not email them. We only want credit for jobs we help bring back.

Is my customer data safe?

Your list stays yours. We use it only to email your past estimate customers for you. We never sell it or share it, and we never use it for another builder.

Do homeowners get to opt out?

Yes. Every email has a clear way to opt out, and we honor it right away. Each email also shows your business address.

Do you send texts?

Not right now. We use email only.

How do I cancel?

Send us an email. There is no contract term. Fees are still owed on jobs from your list that sign within the 12 month window.
